Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Drive Sync is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive. It requests access to Google Drive solely to upload, list and manage the owner's files.

No data from any other person is collected. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
